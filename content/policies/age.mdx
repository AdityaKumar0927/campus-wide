# Age and Eligibility Policy

<Banner />

Campus Wide is for adult members of a campus community.

- You must be at least 17 years old, the age at which a first-year student may be enrolled at most campuses we support, and you attest to this at sign-up. Where the law where you live sets a higher age for consenting to services like this one, that age applies.
- We do not knowingly keep an account for anyone younger. If you believe a member is under 17, report the profile; we remove the account and its data.
- Nothing on the service is age-restricted content, and nothing on the service may be used to buy or sell alcohol, nicotine, cannabis, or medication.
- Members who leave the campus (graduate, withdraw) keep a read-only account until the next term check, after which it is removed.
