# House Rules and Safety

<Banner />

Read this once, tick each line before your first notice, and come back whenever something feels off. The same text is linked from every thread.

## What you agree to

1. **Meet in public campus places, in daylight where you can.** The safe-exchange spots are staffed and public. Never at a residence.
2. **Nothing is bought or sold here.** No payments, no deposits, no payment apps, no moving a deal to another app. Meal credits are never for sale, trade, or loan.
3. **A meal treat only happens with the plan holder present at the register.** Never lend or borrow a campus card, a PIN, or a photo of either.
4. **Block and report.** Block is silent and total. Reports keep the whole thread as evidence and get a human answer within 48 hours.
5. **You are not anonymous.** Your UID identifies you to moderators, and to the university if you break these rules.

## If someone lies, stalks, or keeps messaging

- Block them first. They are not told, and the thread freezes for both of you.
- Report with the category that fits; the thread, both handles, and timestamps are attached automatically.
- If you are in danger, call 911 or campus Public Safety ([PUBLIC SAFETY PHONE]). Campus resources: [TITLE IX CONTACT], [COUNSELLING CONTACT], [DEAN OF STUDENTS CONTACT].
- Export the thread from its page if you want to hand it to Public Safety or the Dean of Students.

## What the platform is and is not

Student-run, not affiliated with or endorsed by the university. It cannot see meal plan balances or any campus system. Moderators are students with a faculty sponsor, not police.

## What is kept, and for how long

Your identity for the life of the account; sign-in events for 90 days; notices until they expire; relay messages for the thread life plus one year; reports and decisions for two years. Deleting your account anonymises what you pinned and purges your identity after 30 days.
