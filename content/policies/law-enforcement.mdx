# Law Enforcement and Campus Requests Policy

<Banner />

This policy explains how we handle requests for member data from police, courts, and campus offices.

## Principles

- We disclose the minimum necessary, only under a valid legal process or a genuine emergency, and only after verifying who is asking.
- We tell the member, unless the law forbids it or telling them would put someone in danger.
- Campus offices are not law enforcement. A request from a Dean of Students, a Title IX office, or Public Safety is treated as a third-party request: we ask for the legal basis, and where a member has consented to escalation in a report, we act on that consent.

## What we can produce

Account identity (campus email, UID, declared name), sign-in metadata for the last 90 days, content the member pinned, relay threads the member took part in, reports and moderation records. We cannot produce meal plan balances, class information, or anything else held by the campus.

## Valid process

A subpoena, court order, or warrant from a court of competent jurisdiction, served at [LEGAL NOTICE ADDRESS] or [LEGAL CONTACT EMAIL]. Requests from outside [JURISDICTION] must come through mutual legal assistance or an equivalent lawful route.

## Emergencies

Where we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to prevent imminent death or serious injury, we may disclose without process. We record every such decision in the audit log and review it afterwards.

## Preservation

On a valid preservation request we keep the named data for 90 days, extendable once.

## Transparency

Aggregate numbers appear in the Content Moderation Transparency Report.
