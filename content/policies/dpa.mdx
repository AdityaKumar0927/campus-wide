# Data Processing Addendum (template for universities)

<Banner />

This template is offered to a university that wishes to adopt Campus Wide officially. Until signed, the service is student-run and the university is not a party.

## Parties and roles

[UNIVERSITY LEGAL NAME] ("Institution") and [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Provider"). The Provider acts as an independent controller for member accounts and as a processor for any data the Institution supplies (for example a verified list of email domains or moderator appointments).

## Subject matter and duration

Provision of a campus notice board to verified members for the term of the agreement, plus the retention periods in the Privacy Notice.

## Nature and purpose

Hosting, displaying, and moderating member content; verifying campus affiliation by email domain; relaying messages between members without disclosing addresses; producing aggregate, non-identifying statistics.

## Categories of data and data subjects

Students, faculty, and staff of the Institution: campus email and UID, declared name, content, sign-in metadata, safety and moderation records, consent records. No special categories are requested; if members volunteer them in content, they are treated as content.

## Provider obligations

Process only on documented instructions; keep personnel bound to confidentiality; apply the technical and organisational measures in the Security Policy; use only the subprocessors on the Subprocessor List and give 30 days notice of changes; assist with data subject requests and impact assessments; delete or return Institution data at the end of the term; make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance, including the audit log for the Institution's members in aggregate.

## Breach notification

Notice to [INSTITUTION SECURITY CONTACT] without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware, with the facts known, likely consequences, and measures taken.
