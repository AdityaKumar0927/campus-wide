# Community Guidelines and Acceptable Use

<Banner />

Everyone here is a real, named person on your campus. Write like they can see you, because they can.

## 1. Be a real person

Use the service as yourself, once. No second accounts, no lending your login, no pretending to be someone else, no bots.

## 2. No harassment, no threats

Do not insult, threaten, intimidate, or demean anyone. Do not target someone because of who they are. Do not post sexual content or anything that sexualises another member.

## 3. No stalking, no unwanted contact

One message until they reply. If someone blocks you, that is the end of it; do not look for another way to reach them. Do not post where someone lives or their schedule.

## 4. No scams, no payments off the board

Nothing is bought or sold through the service. Do not ask for deposits, gift cards, or transfers, and do not move a conversation to another app to get around that. Meal credits are never for sale, trade, or loan.

## 5. Only things you may share

No stolen goods, no weapons, no alcohol or nicotine, no drugs or prescription medication, no counterfeits, no academic work for sale, no tickets for resale above face value, and nothing your housing or dining contract forbids.

## 6. Keep it useful

No spam, no chain notices, no reposting the same thing across spaces. Mark your question answered when it is.

## What happens when you break these

A human reads every report within 48 hours. Depending on the case: a hidden or removed notice, a warning, a suspension with a date, or a ban. Every decision comes with a written statement of reasons and one appeal to a different moderator. Threats and stalking are also pointed to Public Safety, and with your consent as the reporter, to the Dean of Students or the Title IX office.
