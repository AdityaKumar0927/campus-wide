# Meal Sharing Policy

<Banner />

This board exists so a student with a guest meal to spare can treat a fellow student, in person, the way the dining contract already allows. It exists for nothing else.

## What is allowed

- A member who holds a plan with guest meals treats another member to one of those guest meals, at the residential dining hall, with the plan holder present at the register and tapping their own card.
- A member who needs a meal may post a private request that only attested plan holders see.
- Both tap "it happened" afterwards; the holder is credited for helping.

## What is never allowed

Selling, trading, lending, or transferring any meal swipe, guest meal, dining dollars, bonus points, or campus card. Handing over a card, a PIN, or a photo of a card. Asking for anything in return, including a "small tip". Leaving anything "at the desk". Any of these is removed on report and may cost you your account; your campus housing and dining contract almost certainly says it may cost you more.

## Honesty about eligibility

Plan status is self-reported and labelled as such. The service cannot see balances and never asks for screenshots. A false claim costs the other person a walk; two no-show reports suspend your offers for the term.

## The campus decides

The board is switched off until the campus office responsible for dining confirms in writing that guest meals may be used this way. The campus admin re-reads the housing and dining contract each term before switching it back on. The text you tick before every offer quotes the presence and non-transfer rules.
