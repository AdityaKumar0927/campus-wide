## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| Your campus email address and the UID in it | To verify that you belong to the campus and to identify you to moderators if you break the rules | Life of the account, then purged |
| The name you declare once at sign-up, as it appears on your campus ID | So every member is a real, named person to the campus community | Life of the account, then purged |
| Sign-in events: time, device family, coarse location from the IP address | Account-sharing detection and your own session list | 90 days |
| Notices, replies, votes, sign-ups, thank-yous | The service itself | Until expiry or deletion; anonymised on account deletion |
| Relay messages between you and another member | Safety evidence if something goes wrong | Thread life plus one year |
| Reports you file, moderation decisions, appeals | Accountability and legal holds | Two years, pseudonymised after account deletion |
| Consent records (which policy version you accepted, when, from which device) | Proof of acceptance | Life of the account plus one year |
| Your self-reported meal plan attestation | Eligibility for the meal gifting board | The current term only |
| Feedback you send, with the page and browser version if you tick the box | Improving the service | Two years |

We do not collect photographs of people, phone numbers, payment details, or precise location. We never ask for a picture of a campus ID.

## Legal basis

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies: performance of the service you asked for (contract), our legitimate interest in keeping the campus safe (safety evidence, moderation records), and your consent for anything optional (the weekly digest, feedback context, browser models). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.

## Who can see what

- Other verified members of your campus see your display name (given name and family initial), your UID, what you pin, and the counts on your profile. In privacy mode they see initials only.
- Moderators (trusted students with a faculty sponsor) see reports, the evidence attached to them, and your UID.
- The university does not receive data from us unless you file a report and consent to escalation, or a lawful request arrives (see the Law Enforcement and Campus Requests Policy).
- Nobody sees your email address unless you and one other member both choose to share it inside a relay thread.

## Your rights

Export everything about you as a JSON archive and delete your account from Settings at any time. Deletion anonymises what you pinned, purges your identity after a 30-day grace period, and keeps only what the retention table above says we must keep. You may also ask us to correct your declared name (in person, through a moderator) and complain to [SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY] if you believe we have handled your data unlawfully.
