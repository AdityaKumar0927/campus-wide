# Accessibility Statement

<Banner />

Campus Wide aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on every page, at phone and desktop widths, in light and dark mode.

## What we do

- Every build runs automated accessibility checks (axe) at 390 px and 1440 px on the main routes; a failing check fails the build.
- Everything works with a keyboard: skip link, visible focus, a `?` shortcut sheet, no keyboard traps.
- Colour is never the only signal; contrast meets 4.5:1 for text; motion respects the reduced-motion setting and never carries content.
- Forms have labels, errors are announced, and touch targets are at least 24 by 24 px.
- Text scales with your browser settings; there is no horizontal scroll at phone width.

## Known limitations

- Some third-party pieces (the confetti on feedback, the spinning seal) are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
- Documents you download (calendar files, exports) are machine formats.

## Tell us

If something is hard to use, write to [ACCESSIBILITY CONTACT EMAIL] or use the Feedback button on any page. We aim to answer within five working days and to fix confirmed barriers in the next release.
