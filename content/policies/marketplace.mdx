# Marketplace Safety and Prohibited Items

<Banner />

The marketplace is a notice board with tear-off tabs, not a shop. Nothing is paid for through the service.

## How a sale works here

1. You pin a listing with a price (or free), a condition, and a photo.
2. A buyer takes a tab; the conversation runs through the relay. Neither of you sees an email address unless you both choose to share it.
3. You agree on a safe-exchange spot on campus (the list is shown in every thread) and hand over in person.
4. Both tap "it happened". The seller is credited for helping.

## Safety rules

- Meet at the safe-exchange spots only: staffed, public, on campus. Never at a residence.
- Cash in person, or nothing. No deposits, no "hold it for me" transfers, no gift cards, no payment links.
- If a message mentions payment apps or moving off the board, the service shows both people a caution. Trust it.
- Block anyone who pressures you. Report scams and no-shows; the thread is the evidence.

## Prohibited items

Meal swipes, guest meals, dining dollars, bonus points, campus cards, student IDs; alcohol, nicotine and vapes, cannabis, prescription or controlled medication; firearms, ammunition, knives beyond kitchen use; stolen or counterfeit goods; academic work, exam material, essay writing; tickets above face value; animals; anything your campus housing or dining contract forbids. Listings that match these are refused at pinning and removed on report.

## Lost and found hand-offs

Describe found items loosely so the owner can prove it is theirs in the thread. Leave valuable found items with Public Safety or a staffed desk and say so in the notice.
