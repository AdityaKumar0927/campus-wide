# Cookie Policy

<Banner />

Campus Wide uses strictly necessary cookies only. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to opt out of.

## The cookies we set

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| `sb-*-auth-token` | Keeps you signed in (issued by our authentication provider, Supabase) | Up to 30 days, refreshed while you use the service |
| `theme` | Remembers light or dark mode | One year |

## What we do not do

- No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, no fingerprinting.
- Analytics, when a campus enables them, are cookieless and aggregate. They are switched off entirely when your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal.
- Browser-side models (the "Already asked?" box and the harsh-tone nudge) store their files in your browser cache. You can switch them off in Settings; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Global Privacy Control

We treat the GPC signal as an opt-out of any optional data use. Our machine-readable statement is at `/.well-known/gpc.json`.

## Local storage

We keep a few preferences in your browser only: your theme, whether browser models are on, and unsent drafts. They never leave your device.
